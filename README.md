# Decision-Makers Identification Task (ABM Logic)

This repository contains my solution for the **internship entrance task** focused on **Account-Based Marketing (ABM)**.  
The task involves identifying key decision-makers for target companies and justifying their relevance for multi-profile LinkedIn marketing.

## Project Overview
The goal of this project is to carefully identify **five key decision-makers** for each target company to optimize ABM campaigns.  
Each decision-maker is selected based on their role, influence, and impact on marketing, sales, and strategic decisions.

## Decision-Makers Criteria
For each company, the following decision-makers are targeted:

1. **Founder / Co-Founder / CEO**  
   - Primary decision-maker and strategic leader.
2. **CMO / VP Marketing / Head of Growth**  
   - Responsible for marketing strategy and growth initiatives.
3. **Sales Head / VP Sales / Business Development Head**  
   - Key in revenue generation and partnership decisions.
4. **HR Head / Talent Acquisition Lead**  
   - Important for recruitment strategies and team growth.
5. **One Additional CxO / VP (Tech, Strategy, or relevant domain)**  
   - Selected based on specific company needs or domain influence.

Each lead is carefully qualified with a **justification for their selection**, explaining why they are relevant for ABM campaigns.
